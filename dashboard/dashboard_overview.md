# Dashboard Overview

## Purpose
The dashboard serves as a centralized platform that provides real-time insights into the performance and operational status of the Smart City IoT Street Light Traffic System. It enables city administrators, engineers, and stakeholders to monitor various metrics, manage assets, and make informed decisions to optimize the street light operations.

## Features
- **Real-Time Data Display:** View live data from street lights and traffic systems, showing their operational status, energy consumption, and more.
- **Alerts and Notifications:** Receive alerts for outages, maintenance needs, or other critical events affecting the street light infrastructure.
- **Energy Analytics:** Analyze energy consumption patterns and identify opportunities for energy savings and efficiency improvements.
- **Historical Data Access:** Access historical data trends to review system performance over time and aid in future planning.
- **Interactive Maps:** Visualize street light locations and conditions on an interactive map, helping to pinpoint areas needing attention.
- **User Management:** Manage user access and roles within the dashboard to ensure appropriate permissions are maintained.
- **Reporting Tools:** Generate reports to help assess system performance and compliance with safety and legislative standards.
