# Viva Explanation

This document serves as an explanation of the Smart City IoT Street Light Traffic System project. 

## Overview  
The Smart City IoT Street Light Traffic System is designed to manage street lights efficiently using IoT technology. This system aims to reduce energy consumption, improve safety, and provide real-time data for city management.  

## Key Features  
1. **Smart Street Lighting**: Utilizes sensors to adjust brightness based on ambient light and motion detection.  
2. **Data Analytics**: Gathers data on traffic patterns and light usage for better city planning.  
3. **Remote Monitoring**: Provides an interface for city officials to monitor the status of street lights in real-time.  
4. **Energy Efficiency**: Reduces energy costs by ensuring that lights are only on when necessary.

## How It Works  
- **Sensors** detect the presence of vehicles and pedestrians, adjusting the light accordingly.  
- **Communication** between street lights and a central cloud server to handle data processing and storage.  
- **User Interface** for monitoring and controlling the system, ensuring the maintenance of street lights and efficient energy use.

## Conclusion  
The Smart City IoT Street Light Traffic System represents a significant step towards more efficient urban management. By leveraging IoT technologies, cities can become smarter and more responsive to the needs of their residents.
